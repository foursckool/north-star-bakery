Our Story

North Star Bakery started in a small home kitchen with a simple goal: to make the best loaf of bread in town. Today, we are proud to be a staple of the neighborhood, serving families, commuters, and local businesses.

Behind the Scenes

Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that great baking starts with great ingredients. We source our flour from local mills and use only real butter, fresh eggs, and seasonal fruits. We never use artificial preservatives—just time, temperature, and care.
